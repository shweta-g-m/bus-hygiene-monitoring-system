About Smart Bus Hygiene

Our mission, our values, and how we work.

What We Do

Smart Bus Hygiene Monitoring is a real-time passenger feedback platform for public transit systems. Passengers scan a QR code inside the bus to rate cleanliness, flag hygiene issues like bad smells, muddy floors, dirty seats, or vomit — all in under 60 seconds.

Every submission is processed through simple alert logic. The system generates alerts automatically and marks buses as unclean when issues are reported. Admin staff can view, filter, and resolve all issues through the dashboard — closing the loop between passengers and operations.
